# Rikkahub Revised 2.4.8-revised.9

更新内容:

- 完善世界书导入、关键词与正则匹配、递归扫描、来源绑定和角色资料支持；重组条目编辑、检查及测试页面，增加帮助教程和扫描深度参考。
- 增加 MiniMax 语音合成、小米 MiMo 语音合成与识别的可调参数，按模型显示支持的选项，并支持获取和筛选语音模型；改善录音收尾、转写草稿、失败重试、朗读暂停及长文本播放。
- 修复 Gemini 问答工具在部分情况下提示“缺少 questions”的问题，改善内置工具与其他工具同时使用时的兼容性和连续调用。
- 修复长选项列表导致的崩溃、菜单背景异常放大，以及部分网页内容引发的页面切换崩溃；统一世界书、语音和供应商组件的背景、透明度及文字配色。
- 请求日志支持退出应用后保留，并按存储上限自动清理；响应正文和长系统提示词支持分段与折叠阅读，改善背景适配，同时保留完整复制与导出能力。

Updates:

- Improved world-book imports, keyword and regular-expression matching, recursive scanning, source bindings, and character information. Reorganized entry editing, checks, and testing, with new tutorials and scan-depth examples.
- Added adjustable settings for MiniMax speech synthesis and Xiaomi MiMo speech synthesis and recognition, showing supported options for each model and allowing speech-model discovery and filtering. Improved recording completion, editable transcription drafts, retries, reading pauses, and long-text playback.
- Fixed Gemini question prompts sometimes reporting missing “questions,” and improved compatibility and continued use when built-in tools are combined with other tools.
- Fixed crashes caused by long option lists, enlarged menu backgrounds, and page switching with some web content. Applied consistent backgrounds, opacity, and text colors to world-book, speech, and provider controls.
- Request logs now survive app restarts and are automatically pruned at storage limits. Response bodies and long system instructions support segmented, collapsible reading with improved background styling, while preserving full copying and export.
